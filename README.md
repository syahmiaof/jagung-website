## 🌽 Overview & Creative Vision

**jagung-website** (`jagung.syahmiaof.my`) is a high-impact interactive 3D WebGL web experience developed by Muhammad Syahmi, inspired by the award-winning *Pioneer Corn Revolution* digital campaign. It recreates complex environmental storytelling, real-time procedural plant genetics, and atmospheric agricultural simulation using Three.js and choreographed GSAP timelines.

### Key Value Pillars:
- **Functionality**: Multi-chapter scrollytelling structure (Science, Real World Testing, Field Results) featuring interactive stress condition pickers (Drought resistance, High-Wind lodging) and real-time 3D camera transitions.
- **Aesthetic Excellence**: High-definition terrain rendering, organic leaf geometry shaders, volumetric sunlight effects, and tactile custom cursor feedback.
- **Business Impact**: Demonstrates premier digital marketing and interactive product visualization capabilities for agritech, enterprise science, and environmental sustainability sectors.

---

## 🧬 Architectural Architecture & Scene Pipeline

- **Three.js Scene Graph**: Custom procedural mesh generation for crop stalks, root structures, and dynamic wind vertex displacement shaders.
- **GSAP Orchestration**: Multi-stage chapter progression with stateful camera morphing and buttery smooth narrative transitions.
- **Asset Pipeline**: High-density compressed WebP textures and optimized geometry buffers served via edge-ready static delivery.
